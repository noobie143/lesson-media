## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for primary school learners (Classes 1â€“5, ages 5â€“11) in the Eastern Ghats region of Andhra Pradesh. The interface channels the organic warmth and geography of Araku Valley and Paderu through lush botanic greens, solar reward golds, and parchment tones. 

The aesthetic is **Tactile Warmth & Playful Clarity**. It eschews sterile institutional surfaces in favor of chunky, high-affordance elements designed for low-friction touchscreens, budget Android devices, and emerging digital literacies. The emotional tone must evoke safety, encouraging companionship, and tactile celebrationâ€”making every learning micro-action feel as tangible as a wooden stamp or village school reward badge.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in nature-inspired hues optimized for high legibility under outdoor sun conditions on mid-tier mobile screens.

- **Primary (`#155D3B` - Forest Green):** Anchor color for primary actions, bottom navigation active states, and major task completion containers.
- **Deep Accent (`#10452F` - Deep Forest Green):** Reserved for high-contrast headlines, critical Telugu typography, and anchor borders to maximize contrast against ivory backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#287D78` - Support Teal):** Dedicated to auditory anchors, pronounce-aloud prompts, listening assistive tools, and auxiliary tags.
- **Tertiary (`#F2CB55` - Reward Gold):** Gamification currencyâ€”stars, streaks, level progression milestones, and active quest rings.
- **Background Neutral (`#FFFBEF` - Ivory):** Soft daylight canvas that reduces blue-light strain without producing the harsh glare of raw white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-affordance elevated cards, question sheets, and dialogue tiles.
- **Success & State Mint (`#DDF5E8`):** Micro-celebration fills, correct answer pills, and completed lesson nodes.
- **Secondary Text (`#666666`):** Metainformation, subheadings, and contextual hints exceeding WCAG AA criteria.
- **Critical Alert (`#C62828`):** Direct, unambiguous error and retry states.
